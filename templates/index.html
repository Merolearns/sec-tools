passcheck

Password strength analyzer. Scores a password 0–100, estimates its entropy and rough brute-force crack times, and flags common passwords (including leetspeak variants), short length, low variety, and common sequences — all checked locally.

port-scout

Multithreaded TCP port scanner with banner grabbing. Give it a host and a port list or the common-ports preset, and it reports each port's state, best-guess service, and any banner the service sends back.

Scan a host →

Also on the command line: python3 port_scout.py <target> --common
